01THE RESEARCH LANDSCAPE

Evidence, not
assumption.

Capital can move faster than the trust required to allocate it responsibly.

Community Trust Infrastructure (CTI) explores how real-world activity can become credible, portable evidence without imposing a universal scoring system, a single technology, or external control over community decisions.
