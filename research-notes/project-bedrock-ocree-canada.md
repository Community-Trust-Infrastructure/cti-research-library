# Project Bedrock — Ocree and Canadian Private-Markets Infrastructure

**Date of announcement:** November 17, 2026  
**Status:** Announced pilot / implementation watch. Not independently validated and not evidence of CTI implementation.  
**Primary source:** [Canadian Securities Exchange announcement](https://primary.thecse.com/news/ocree-partners-with-the-canadian-securities-exchange-apex-group-and-tetra-digital-group-to-launch-a-canadian-pilot-to-create-a-private-markets-digital-ecosystem/)

## What was announced

Ocree Corporation, the Canadian Securities Exchange (CSE), Apex Group (through Tokeny) and Tetra Digital Group announced the next phase of **Project Bedrock**, a Canadian pilot exploring distribution of tokenized private real-estate securities through existing exchange, dealer and custody infrastructure.

The proposed infrastructure combines:
- Tokenization via Tokeny;
- Ocree's **Ocree Asset Rating and Disclosure System (OARDS)** for standardized, AI-assisted diligence, disclosure and compliance;
- Tetra's Canadian-dollar **CADD** stablecoin as an intended settlement mechanism; and
- Existing regulated market infrastructure, with secondary-market trading envisaged for a subsequent phase.

The announcement says the pilot will operate under existing Canadian private-placement securities requirements. Ocree Capital Inc. is described as a registered exempt market dealer. Discussions with OSC staff and an application to CIRO InnovateSafe are **not** equivalent to regulatory approval of the pilot.

## CTI research question

> As financial assets become tokenized and settlement becomes programmable, how do we ensure that the evidence supporting investment decisions is independently verifiable, and that authority and accountability remain clearly defined?

## CTI interpretation: adjacent, not equivalent

OARDS is described as standardizing disclosure, due diligence and compliance information for financial-market participants. Standardized presentation does not, by itself, establish that underlying evidence is independently verified, that competing interpretations can be contested, or that decision authority is appropriately distributed.

**CTI's broader research focus** is community-generated evidence, provenance, contextual attestations, validation, contestability and legitimate governance authority. CTI is not an asset-tokenization platform, securities disclosure system, custodian or settlement rail.

The comparison is useful precisely because the systems address related but distinct layers: information and compliance for private securities versus portable operational trust and accountable decision-making across contexts.

## What to monitor

1. Whether the pilot completes issuance and distribution of private-market securities, and what is actually demonstrated.
2. Whether CADD settlement is executed in practice, under which custody and legal arrangements, and with what operational controls.
3. What evidence supports OARDS disclosures, how source data is verified, and whether errors can be challenged and corrected.
4. How legal responsibilities are divided among issuer, dealer, exchange, custodian, tokenization provider and settlement provider.
5. Whether subsequent secondary trading demonstrates liquidity, compliant transfers and accountable exception handling.
6. Whether any independent evaluation or regulatory findings become public.

**Evidence limitation:** The November 17 announcement describes objectives, partners and intended functionality; it does not establish pilot success, independent verification of disclosures, realized liquidity or endorsement of CTI.
