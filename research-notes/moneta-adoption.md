# Moneta — stablecoin adoption in emerging markets

**Source:** Michał Moneta (2026), *Stablecoin Adoption in Emerging Markets: A TAM-Based Model of Digital Finance Use*

## Central argument
This conceptual research brings technology-adoption factors into the discussion of stablecoin use in emerging markets. Practical adoption depends on more than the existence of a functioning transfer mechanism: infrastructure, perceived usefulness, trust, literacy, incentives and actual ability to convert value can all matter.

## Evidence and status
**Conceptual working paper / preprint; full bibliographic and methodological review pending.** The CTI register describes a synthesis of prior literature rather than a field experiment demonstrating causal effects. Its framework should not be presented as measured proof of stablecoin adoption outcomes.

## Critical interpretation
Distinguish access, intention to use, initial use and sustained beneficial use. A person may receive digital value but lack a safe off-ramp, affordable connectivity, local liquidity or the confidence to transact independently.

## CTI architecture implications
- Treat **operational usability** as a testable requirement, separate from technical interoperability.
- Design for low-friction, familiar interfaces and human support.
- Capture evidence of actual user experience, failed attempts and recourse, not merely successful transfers.
- Keep the trust layer usable with multiple settlement methods.

## Limitations and counterarguments
Technology-adoption models can overemphasize individual intentions while underplaying coercion, institutional exclusion, gendered barriers, regulatory constraints and informal trust relationships. Context-specific field evidence is necessary.

## Questions for applied testing
1. Can community members complete the full receive–use–cash-out workflow without expert intervention?
2. Which failures are caused by technology, local liquidity or institutional rules?
3. Does the system reduce administrative burden for communities, or merely move that burden elsewhere?
