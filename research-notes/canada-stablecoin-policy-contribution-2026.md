# Policy contribution — Canadian Stablecoin Regulations

**Author:** Shannon Lanigan, in an individual capacity as an independent researcher developing CTI.  
**Document date:** November 24, 2026.  
**Consultation:** [Canada Gazette, Part I, October 24, 2026](https://gazette.gc.ca/rp-pr/p1/2026/2026-10-24/html/reg1-eng.html).  
**Status:** Final consultation response prepared for email submission; dispatch and official receipt not independently confirmed. This record documents a policy contribution prepared for consultation, not government endorsement, adoption or demonstrated policy influence.

## Recommendations

1. Maintain strong reserve integrity, redemption, custody and independent verification requirements.
2. Preserve currency-neutral regulation while supporting Canadian-dollar settlement capacity and domestic regulatory sovereignty.
3. Recognize humanitarian, philanthropic and community-finance applications as areas for supervised innovation.
4. Clarify regulatory obligations for decentralized governance and emerging organizational structures.
5. Support interoperable evidence, attestations and accountability alongside settlement infrastructure.
6. Maintain proportionate AML/CFT, sanctions, privacy and national security safeguards.
7. Establish accessible regulatory experimentation pathways for smaller organizations and public-benefit initiatives.
8. Measure success through adoption, resilience, consumer outcomes, interoperability and demonstrable public benefit.

## Relevance to CTI

The response translates CTI research into policy recommendations while maintaining technology and provider neutrality. It distinguishes transaction integrity from evidence integrity and decision authority. CTI is an emerging reference architecture, not a validated regulatory standard. The submission is personal and does not imply endorsement by AIFS or its partners.
