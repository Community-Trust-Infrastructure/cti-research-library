EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY

How we curate.

This library is an evolving, curated reading index rather than a systematic literature review. It brings together relevant scholarship, institutional reports, technical standards, policy and applied research.

Principles

  1. Prefer original publications and institutional sources over secondary commentary.
  2. Keep source summaries separate from CTI editorial interpretations.
  3. Distinguish preliminary research, institutional announcements and tested outcomes.
  4. Record provenance and corrections through Git history.
  5. Do not invent publication metadata or imply institutional endorsement of CTI.
  6. Remain technology-neutral across blockchain, payment rails and governance systems.

Current status

This first release is a starter selection from the wider CTI working bibliography, not the complete literature index. Some publication dates are recorded by year until detailed citation review is complete. The collection will grow as sources are reconciled and verified.

Suggest a source

Use GitHub Issues to suggest a publication, flag an error or propose a correction. Please include an original URL and publication details.
